## Recipe for cooking Omena

Ingredients
- Omena 500g
- cooking Oil 
- Tomatoes
- Onions 

Procedure 
- Boil the omena for 15 minutes
- Wash the omena and place it on a seperate plate
- Put oil in your sufuria
- Deep fry the omena till golden brown
- Add your onions and wait till golden brown
- Add Tomatoes 
- Allow to cook for 20 minutes.
